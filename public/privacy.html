4. Email Privacy: How Relay Mail Works

Relay Mail is an email client, not an email hosting provider.

It connects to external email providers such as Gmail to let you access and manage your existing mailbox.

Your mailbox remains with your email provider. It does not become a RelayHQ mailbox.

4.1 Your Emails Are Not Stored on RelayHQ Servers

Relay Mail retrieves email information directly from your connected email provider when needed.

We do not persistently store your:

  • Email message bodies.
  • Inbox and sent messages.
  • Draft contents.
  • Email attachments.
  • Message history.
  • Mailbox conversations.

Email information may be temporarily processed in memory or transmitted through the systems necessary to fulfil your requests.

However, RelayHQ does not maintain a persistent duplicate mailbox on its servers.

We do not intentionally store mailbox contents in persistent application databases, analytics systems, server logs, or backups.

4.2 Email Ownership

Your emails, attachments, drafts, and communications remain under the control of your email provider.

Connecting your account to Relay Mail does not transfer ownership of your mailbox or its contents to RelayHQ.

4.3 Email Actions

Depending on your permissions, Relay Mail may allow you to:

  • Read and search messages.
  • Compose and send emails.
  • Organise your mailbox.
  • Access attachments.
  • Perform supported email-management actions.

These operations are performed through your connected email provider.

4.4 Email Provider Availability

Relay Mail depends on external providers for mailbox functionality.

Access may be affected by provider outages, API restrictions, account limitations, expired tokens, or changes to third-party permissions.

4.5 Remote Email Content and Tracking

Email messages may contain tracking pixels, remote images, and externally hosted resources.

Loading these resources may disclose technical information, such as your IP address, approximate location, or message-opening activity, to the sender or external provider.

Opening links or downloading attachments may also disclose information independently of Relay Mail.

Where remote-content privacy controls are available, their operation and limitations will be explained within the product.
